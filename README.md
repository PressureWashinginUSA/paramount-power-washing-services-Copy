## Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing

Not every exterior surface should be cleaned with high pressure. Pressure washing is useful for suitable hard surfaces, such as concrete, while soft washing uses a lower-pressure application and appropriate cleaning solutions for compatible surfaces.

Selecting the right method helps reduce the risk of surface damage and supports effective cleaning. Roofs, siding, painted materials, and other delicate areas should be assessed before work begins.
